# PromptVault — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-27_

PromptVault is designed to be private by default.

## What we collect

**Nothing.** PromptVault does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal
data or usage data. There are no analytics, no trackers, no advertising, and no
remote servers involved in the core product.

## Where your data lives

All of your snippets, folders, tags, and settings are stored **locally on your
own device** using the browser's built-in `chrome.storage.local` API. This data
never leaves your computer unless you explicitly export it to a file yourself.

## Permissions we use, and why

- **storage** — to save your snippets and settings locally on your device.
- **activeTab** and **scripting** — to insert a snippet into the text field of
  the page you are actively using, only when you trigger an insertion (the
  keyboard shortcut or a click in the popup). PromptVault ships no
  background/always-on content script and requests no broad site access
  (`host_permissions`) — it does not read page contents in the background
  and is never present on a page you have not just explicitly invoked it
  on.

## Payments (Pro)

If and when a paid "Pro" tier is offered, payment will be handled by a
third-party payment processor (ExtensionPay / Stripe). In that case only the
information required to process the transaction and validate your license is
processed by that provider under their own privacy policy. PromptVault itself
still does not receive or store your payment details.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL and the
